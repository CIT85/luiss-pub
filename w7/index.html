Kukulkan

Kukulkan feathered serpent

The Feathered Serpent deity of the Yucatec Maya

  • Creator deity and bringer of rain, wind, and life in Yucatec Maya mythology.
  • Bridges the realms of earth, sky, and the underworld.
  • At Chichén Itzá, the equinox sun casts a serpent shadow down the steps of his pyramid.
  • Closely associated with the planet Venus, wisdom, and cyclical renewal.
